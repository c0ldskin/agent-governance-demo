# disability support card: eligibility rules (illustrative, for the demo only)

These rules are a simplified stand-in written for the demo. They are not official criteria.

1. The applicant is a citizen, or a resident with a valid national ID.
2. The applicant has a long-term condition in one of these categories: physical, visual, hearing,
   speech and communication, intellectual, autism spectrum, psychosocial, or multiple.
   Temporary conditions, such as an injury expected to heal, do not qualify.
3. A medical report from a licensed health facility confirms the condition and category.
4. The medical report is dated within the last 12 months.
5. Required documents: national ID copy, personal photo, medical report.

If a document is missing or the medical report is out of date, ask for it rather than rejecting.
